# assets/

## Headshot

Place your headshot here as `photo.jpg`.

The resume template includes an oval-cropped version at the top of the sidebar.

**Recommended specs:**
- Format: JPEG or PNG (rename to `photo.jpg`)
- Size: at least 400×400px
- Aspect ratio: roughly square or portrait (the oval crop handles it)
- Background: plain/neutral works best

**If you don't want a photo:** just don't add the file. The `\ovalphoto` macro skips it automatically and the layout compiles cleanly.

## Company / school logos (optional)

Each experience, education, and achievement entry in the resume reserves an 11mm logo slot. Fill them by dropping files here named to match the template's entries:

- `logo_company1.png`, `logo_company2.png`, ... — experience entries
- `logo_school1.png`, `logo_school2.png` — education entries
- `logo_award1.png`, ... — achievement rows

PNG or JPG both work. Square-ish logos with transparent backgrounds look best. A missing logo degrades to blank reserved space — alignment never shifts and compilation never breaks.

> All images in this folder are excluded from git tracking (see `.gitignore`) — your photo and logos stay on your machine.
