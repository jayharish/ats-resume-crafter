# assets/

Place your headshot here as `photo.jpg`.

The resume template will include an oval-cropped version in the top of the sidebar.

**Recommended specs:**
- Format: JPEG or PNG (rename to `photo.jpg`)
- Size: at least 400×400px
- Aspect ratio: roughly square or portrait (the oval crop handles it)
- Background: plain/neutral works best

**If you don't want a photo:**  
Remove the `\ovalphoto` call from `outputs/resume_latest.tex` and adjust the sidebar `\vspace{8pt}` at the top accordingly.

> `photo.jpg` is excluded from git tracking (see `.gitignore`).
